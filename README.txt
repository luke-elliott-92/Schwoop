Schwoop Scorekeeper — install on your phone

1. Put this whole folder online at an https address (for example, drag the folder onto Netlify Drop, then sign in and claim the site so it stays online).
2. Open that address on your phone.
   iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
   Android (Chrome): tap the menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
3. Open Schwoop from your home screen. It runs full screen, keeps your scores on the phone, and works without a connection after the first visit.
